HOW TO ADD PRODUCT PHOTOS  —  MAHRU
============================================

Every product has its OWN folder inside:
    Frontend/public/images/products/

Open a product's folder and paste its photos, named simply:
    1.jpg   <- main photo (used on cards + shown first)
    2.jpg   <- extra angle (optional)
    3.jpg   <- extra angle (optional)
    4.jpg ... up to 6.jpg

That's it. Refresh the site and the photos appear automatically —
no code editing. Whatever numbers you add show up; missing ones are
skipped. If a product has no photos yet, it shows an elegant fabric
swatch instead, so nothing ever looks broken.

Format: .jpg   |   Portrait ~1000x1333 looks best   |   keep under ~300KB
